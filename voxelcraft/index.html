Getting started

Punch a tree to collect logs, turn them into planks and sticks in your inventory (E), build a crafting table and make a wooden pickaxe. Mine stone for better tools, smelt iron ore in a furnace and place torches before night falls — monsters spawn in the dark!

Inventory: click to pick up / place, right-click to split, shift-click (or double-click) to quick-move, drag to move.
